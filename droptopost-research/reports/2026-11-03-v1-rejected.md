### B1-9 · Story 92 · Evidence Medium · Evergreen
**SHE SAID HER JACKET WASN'T WATERPROOF. THE NORTH FACE SENT A NEW ONE BY HELICOPTER.**
- **Hook:** A soaked hiker told the brand she didn't want a refund. She wanted a new jacket delivered to the top of a mountain trail, and it arrived.
- **What happened:** In late 2023 American hiker Jennifer (Jenn) Jensen got drenched on the Hooker Valley Track in New Zealand's Aoraki/Mount Cook National Park. She posted a TikTok saying her North Face jacket had failed. She asked the brand to redesign it and "express deliver" one to Hooker Valley Lake, "where I will be waiting". The North Face replied with its own video: a staffer grabs a red jacket from its Queenstown store, boards a helicopter and hands it to her at the lake.
- **Biggest twist:** The complaint was a joke. The brand answered the joke literally, and the complaint became its best ad of the year.
- **Why people will watch:** A problem everyone has had, a cheeky demand and an absurd, generous payoff.
- **Why our buyers care:** A fast, funny reply video turned a public complaint into praise. It's a content format, not a budget.
- **Real business results:** Unknown. No sales impact reported, and no statement on whether the jacket was redesigned.
- **Audience evidence:** Jensen's video ~11.5M views, the brand's reply ~4.5M (B&T; other reports say "over 4 million").
- **Original visuals:** Jensen's TikTok and The North Face's TikTok reply, both embedded in the ODT and B&T coverage. Direct post URLs weren't captured.
- **Dates:** Late Nov – early Dec 2023.
- **Sources:** [Otago Daily Times](https://www.odt.co.nz/regions/queenstown/north-faces-reaction-nz-jacket-complaint-goes-viral) · [B&T](https://www.bandt.com.au/north-face-choppers-in-jacket-to-furious-customer-in-possible-marketing-save-of-2023/) · [YPulse](https://www.ypulse.com/newsfeed/2023/12/05/the-north-face-turned-a-viral-tiktok-of-a-customer-complaint-into-a-major-marketing-win/)

- **Hook:** People took one sip of a purple milkshake, then filmed themselves "dead" on the floor.

- **Audience evidence:** Billions of TikTok views for the trend (company and press figures, not checked on the platform).
- **Original visuals:** The #GrimaceShake TikTok videos (thousands of originals) and McDonald's own posts.
- **Dates:** Promo from 12 Jun 2023 · Q2 results Jul 2023.

- **Audience evidence:** Not available.

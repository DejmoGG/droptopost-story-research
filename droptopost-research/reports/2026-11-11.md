### 2026-11-11-B-1 · Candidate 7.5/10 · Recent (7–8 Nov, still unfolding) · Evidence High
**A FOOD FESTIVAL PROMISED STALLHOLDERS 50,000 VISITORS. A FEW HUNDRED CAME. ONE STALL HAD COOKED 200 KG OF CHICKEN, AND THE ORGANISERS DELETED THEIR SOCIAL ACCOUNTS.**
- **What happened:** "Local Food Fight in Tottori", an East-vs-West regional food sales battle in Kurayoshi, Tottori, ran on 7–8 Nov 2026. It was organised by an executive committee run by TOSU LLC (representative Ami Matsubayashi), with YouTuber Sayoto as co-chair, and promoted with a target of 50,000 visitors. Stallholders paid a ¥55,000 fee. Stallholders told FNN and NTV that around 300–500 people came on day one; Livedoor reports under 2,000 over two days. One stall that prepared 200 kg of chicken sold a few kilos (over ¥300,000 lost on ingredients, per coki); a local sake brewery reports about ¥700,000 lost; some stalls took about ¥10,000 on day one and pulled out of day two.
- **The turn:** After the event the organisers deleted their social accounts and stopped answering. Their representative then told NTV that 50,000 "was a target, not a promise".
- **Payoff:** Not yet. On 11 Nov the organisers began posting again; Sayoto apologised and promised an explanation by the end of November. The event contractor disputes the organisers' account (per coki; not confirmed elsewhere).
- **Why anyone would watch:** A festival with stalls, a stage and food for 50,000, and nobody there.
- **Why a business owner cares:** The stallholders paid for a promise made in a promo post; the only proof of the crowd was the organiser's own marketing.
- **Scores:** Entertainment 8 · Narrative 7 · Business 8 · Evidence 8 · Freshness 10
- **How it unfolds:** (1) Organisers sell stalls on a "50,000 visitors" pitch. (2) The weekend comes; the site is near-empty and food is thrown away. (3) Organisers delete their accounts and go quiet. (4) TV crews arrive; stallholders demand an explanation. (5) The organisers resurface with an apology that blames others (pending).
- **Result:** **Reported** (FNN, NTV, Livedoor): official visitor count unknown; stallholder losses as stated by stallholders (¥300,000+, ~¥700,000). No legal action reported yet.

### 2026-11-11-B-2 · Candidate 7/10 · Recent (6 Nov) · Evidence High
**A PIZZA CHAIN'S AD ACCOUNT STARTED MAKING ITS OWN ANIME ADS. NOBODY AT THE COMPANY KNEW, UNTIL FANS NOTICED THE CHARACTERS LOOKED WRONG.**
- **What happened:** PIZZA-LA, a major Japanese pizza-delivery chain run by Four Seeds, ran a licensed collaboration with the anime *Dr. STONE* (1 Oct – 1 Nov 2026). Viewers noticed that its TikTok ads showed the characters in poses, expressions and styles that weren't in the official artwork, and asked whether AI had been used.
- **The turn:** It had, but not by anyone at PIZZA-LA. On 6 Nov the company said staff had set up the campaign with TikTok's AI automatic ad-generation and delivery feature still switched on, without realising; the platform's AI generated and ran video the anime's production committee had never seen or approved.
- **Payoff:** PIZZA-LA pulled the ad, paused all its TikTok advertising, apologised to the production committee and apologised for the late apology. Cost: unknown.
- **Why anyone would watch:** A machine made an unlicensed cartoon ad for a pizza company, and the company found out from fans.
- **Why a business owner cares:** One default setting in an ad dashboard can publish things you never made under your name.
- **Scores:** Entertainment 6 · Narrative 6 · Business 8 · Evidence 8 · Freshness 10

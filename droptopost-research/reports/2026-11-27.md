### 2026-11-27-B-1 · Candidate 7/10 · This month (18 Oct → 26 Nov 2026) · Evidence Medium
**A FAMOUS ACTRESS SAW HERSELF ON A SALES VIDEO, SELLING FACE CREAM SHE HAD NEVER HEARD OF. IT WASN'T HER. AT 3 A.M. SHE WENT AFTER THEM.**
- **What happened:** On 18 Oct, actress Wu Yue (吴越) posted on Weibo in the early hours naming the short-video account "花姐美肤日记" (Hua Jie's skincare diary). It had used AI face-swap and voice cloning to make "her" recommend a cheap skincare product in the first person ("star's pick, cheaper than 618 or Double 11"). The same account had faked Olympic champions Deng Yaping and Guo Jingjing the same way. WeChat Channels banned the account permanently.
- **The turn:** Reporters who followed up found sellers offering custom deepfake celebrity sales videos for about ¥1,000 a minute from a single photo (Sohu investigation). Wu said on CCTV her worry was fans actually buying; her own complaints through the platform had failed.
- **Payoff:** About five weeks later (26 Nov) the brand behind the product published an apology, took the content down and promised full refunds on the related orders; Wu's studio accepted the apology (Sina Finance headline report; not yet confirmed by a second outlet).
- **Why anyone would watch:** Imagine finding a video of yourself selling something you've never touched, in your own voice.
- **Why a business owner cares:** A ¥1,000 AI clone sold product for a few weeks and then cost every sale in refunds, plus the brand's name.
- **Scores:** Entertainment 7 · Narrative 7 · Business 7 · Evidence 6 · Freshness 8
- **How it unfolds:** (1) An account posts AI "Wu Yue" selling skincare. (2) Wu calls it out on Weibo at night. (3) Platform bans the account; earlier fake Olympians surface. (4) Reporters find a ¥1,000-a-minute deepfake market. (5) The brand apologises and refunds every order.
- **Result:** Account banned (WeChat Channels, reported by Yicai, GMW). Brand refunds on all related orders (reported by Sina Finance, 26 Nov; amounts unknown). Sales made before the ban: unknown.

### 2026-11-19-B-1 · Candidate 7.5/10 · This month (5 Nov 2026) · Evidence Medium
**A MAN POSTED ONE ANGRY TIKTOK ABOUT HIS HOME BUILDER. IT HAS NOW COST HIM MORE THAN A MILLION DOLLARS.**
- **What happened:** In Melbourne, Singh Homes terminated Gurvinderpal Singh's building contract on 12 Feb 2024 after disputes over payments and workmanship. He posted a Punjabi-language TikTok attacking the builder; it reached about 220,000 views, mostly in Melbourne's Punjabi community, and spread on WhatsApp. A jury found it conveyed that the directors were dodgy builders who neglected customers and made unreasonable payment demands; comments added that the company was going bust.
- **The turn:** The builder told the court about 12 prospective customers walked away after the video, and Singh kept it online until around July 2025 despite repeated requests to remove it, which earned aggravated damages.
- **Payoff:** The company was awarded **A$673,000** (injurious falsehood, misleading conduct) and each of the two directors **A$105,000** for defamation (28 Oct): **A$883,000** in damages, with costs and interest expected to pass **A$1 million** (SmartCompany, 5 Nov). Reports differ on which parts the Court of Appeal changed.
- **Why anyone would watch:** Everyone has wanted to post an angry review. This is what one cost.
- **Why a business owner cares:** One customer's video in a tight-knit community cost a builder a dozen jobs, and the builder fought back and won.

- **How it unfolds:** (1) Builder ends the contract. (2) Customer posts an angry TikTok in Punjabi; it spreads through WhatsApp. (3) Twelve would-be clients cancel; he refuses to take it down. (4) Jury and judge: A$883,000 plus costs, over A$1M.
- **Result:** Reported via SmartCompany, Fenwick Elliot Grace, Cornwalls (law-firm notes): damages A$883,000; total over A$1M expected. The 12 lost customers is the builder's claim to the court. Appeal outcome: reports conflict.

The owners posted their account; Chiang said sorry on 8 Nov, then asked why other shops didn't have the same problem.

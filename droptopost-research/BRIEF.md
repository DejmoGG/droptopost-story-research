# REPORT B — DropToPost audience stories

**Mission:** "The unbelievable stories behind how businesses attract attention, win customers, and grow."
**This is the owner's highest commercial priority.**

**Viewers we want:** agency owners, entrepreneurs, business owners, SaaS founders and creators. These are people who could later buy DropToPost (content made without filming, AI avatars, automated posting). Every story should make one of them think "I need attention like that" or "that could happen to my brand".

Run procedure, shared rules, scoring and card format: root `CLAUDE.md`. Format reference: `reports/2026-11-03.md`.

## Categories (up to 10 each, ranked)

**B1. Viral marketing miracles & disasters.** Businesses saved by one video, surprising campaigns, brand disasters and backlash, unconventional ads, brands that answered a viral moment brilliantly (or terribly), growth through social media.

**B2. AI & content production.** AI avatars and AI influencers, businesses making content without filming, AI-generated ads (and the backlash), automated marketing, solo founders replacing studios or agencies, AI content that fooled or enraged people. **Not:** model benchmarks, product launches, funding news without a human story, generic "AI will change marketing" pieces.

**B3. Small businesses winning through content.** Local shops, trades, restaurants and one-person brands that got customers, beat competitors or transformed the business with entertaining content. Prefer real before/after numbers (queues, sales, bookings, followers → revenue).

## What "good" looks like (calibration examples, NOT to be reused)
These stories were found earlier and are listed in the global index as `REF-*`. Never present them as new. They show the bar:
- A brewery created a fictional AI woman; customers hated her, and her videos got huge attention.
- A struggling 19-year-old restaurant owner posted one TikTok and customers came from across the country.
- An AI app reportedly got millions of views before anyone could download it.
- Dove turned honest Reddit reviews into a major ad campaign.
- A burned-out car with a Stanley cup still holding ice, and the brand replaced her car.
- A chocolate maker sold almost nothing until a TikTok made Dubai chocolate a global craze.
- A creator's silly Dr Pepper song became a national ad.
- An ordinary designer explained a suitcase on a whiteboard and got millions of views.
- An electric-truck firm rolled a truck downhill and filmed it as "working".
- An employee wired $25M after a video meeting full of deepfakes.

Common thread: **a person, one moment, a twist, a number at the end.** Explainable in two sentences.

## Card extras for Report B
- **Original visuals** matters most here: link the original TikTok / YouTube / ad / post / brand reply wherever possible.
- **Why our audience cares** must say what an agency or business owner learns (the attention mechanism), in one line.
- **Audience signals**: real views, likes, comments, Reddit threads or Trends signals with the source. Never estimate.

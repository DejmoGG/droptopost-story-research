# Published video performance (Report B learning loop)

The owner (or a future automation) adds one row per published video. **Only real numbers from the platforms. Never estimate or invent.** Leave a cell empty when it's unknown.

The research routine reads this file before choosing stories. Once there are 5 or more rows, it notes in the report header which story types (B1/B2/B3, disaster or miracle, AI or human, fresh or evergreen) bring the **right** viewers: high watch time and retention, saves, qualified website visits and leads. Views alone don't count.

## B-14 · Lacy / Zauo Shibuya QR-code orders (9.5)

**Verified (2+ sources):** 28 Oct 2026, Zauo Shibuya, Tokyo (diners catch their own fish). The table's ordering QR code appeared on Lacy's Twitch stream; viewers placed prank orders. Zauo's figure: ¥1.96M of prank orders; Lacy's on-stream estimate ~¥1.6M. Zauo says staff cooked some dishes before spotting the prank, discarded them, and charged only for what was eaten. Lacy later admitted: "I'm dumb and thought it was funny."
**Watch out for:**
- On stream Lacy said he told staff to **give the extra food to local families** (InternetH0F, corruptmp4, a Chinese report says "shared with other diners"). Zauo's statement says the cooked prank dishes were **thrown away**. Don't state either as fact without the restaurant's own words.
- Zauo's statement is known through screenshots on X (translated). The original Instagram post wasn't found; look for it on Zauo Shibuya's Instagram before filming.
- A receipt for about ¥500,000 is visible on stream (Dexerto). What Lacy finally paid is unconfirmed.

## B-13 · Mo's Chicken Hot Pot (9)

**New since the card: the third act (Jimu News / 极目新闻, 2 Oct 2026):**
- Before: about 10 tables a day, time for karaoke and morning walks (HK01). Peak: about **3,000 customers a day** and about **180 staff** at the main shop. Now: **30-odd staff**, the waiting shelter is gone (one rain shelter became a snack stall), lunch about 60% full on 1 Oct; 100+ chickens a day on weekdays, 200+ at weekends.
- Branches opened in **Yunfu, Foshan, Shanghai and Nanning**. No more expansion planned "for now".
- When his own chickens ran out he switched to chickens from Wens (a big supplier); all branches except Guangxi and Shanghai are back on his own chickens.
- **He has paid off a debt of over 1.8 million yuan** from a failed fish-pond investment, and took a bank loan to build a new 100+ mu (~7 ha) chicken farm in Yunfu.
- Douyin shop: 171,700 items sold by 1 Oct; his "Uncle Mo's" soup base sold 100,000+.
- His line now: "Treat the traffic with a normal heart. Get the quality right and you won't lack business."
- In July (Sixth Tone) he had a livestream team and said he couldn't afford to stop; Jimu lists "livestreaming for money" among the criticisms but doesn't say he still streams. Check before saying he does.
**Date correction:** Chinese outlets date Liu Yuxin's video **25 Mar 2026** (Jimu, Douyin page); Sixth Tone says 1 Apr. Use 25 March (the original's date).
**Other corrections:** his wife denied a viral "collapsed from exhaustion" photo (she says it was AI-generated) and said the account trashing the chicken isn't the owner's. Don't use either.

## B-15 · Xibei vs Luo Yonghao (9)

**Verified sequence (Chinese originals):**
- **10 Sep 2025, 16:55**: Luo posts on Weibo after a meal: "almost all pre-made, and so expensive", and calls for a law forcing restaurants to label pre-made food (NBD).
- **11 Sep**: Jia Guolong says "not one dish" Luo ordered is pre-made under the national definition, promises to sue, says the 370+ restaurants' kitchens open from 12 Sep, and launches a "Luo Yonghao menu" (Beijing News, 36Kr). Luo posts a 100,000-yuan reward for evidence.
- **12 Sep**: Xibei's open letter: "Luo's accusations are untrue" (IT Home).
- **14 Sep**: Xibei **stops** letting customers into kitchens ("to avoid disrupting operations"); glass windows only (HK01).
- **15 Sep**: apology; more prep moved back into stores (KrASIA).
- **15 Jan 2026**: 102 closures confirmed (~30%, ~4,000 jobs). **16 Jan**: both Weibo accounts muted. Jia steps down as CEO of the main brand.
- Store count reported to have fallen from nearly 400 to about 226 (Sichuan outlet cbgc; one source, label it "reported").
**Must not say:** that the kitchens proved illegal or unsafe food. The fight was about freshness, disclosure, the definition of "pre-made", and what customers expect for the price. Frozen ingredients and central kitchens are legal.
**Nice beat:** right after the fight began, the branch where Luo ate was fuller than usual and a diner shouted "came specially to support Mr Jia" (YouTube short, below). The support didn't last.

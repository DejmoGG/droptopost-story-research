# Historical research (separate from the daily reports)

A backlog of true business stories from 1900–2025, mostly the last 50 years, built in batches. It runs **beside** the daily Report A/B research and never replaces it: the daily routine is unchanged.

| File | What |
|---|---|
| [backlog.md](backlog.md) | every lead ever screened, with a status (target 300–500 over several batches) |
| [batches/](batches/) | one file per batch: the researched stories with full cards |
| [history.md](history.md) | one row per researched story; the owner edits the Status column |

Stories also go into `shared/global-story-index/index.md` (Report `H`), so neither the daily runs nor later batches repeat them.

## What a batch does
1. Screen leads quickly (year, country, industry, does it have the shape, is it known) into the backlog.
2. Research the strongest 20–30: at least two credible sources each, found directly (news archives, court records, local-history encyclopedias, brand/university archives, original ads and footage). Top-10 blogs are leads only.
3. Write the cards in `batches/<date>-batch-<n>.md` and put them on the Story Desk under **Historical**.

## The standard
Surprising, bizarre, funny or moving; understood in seconds; real; credibly evidenced; visual; relevant to business. A reversal helps but isn't required. Range across industries, decades, countries and story shapes. **Never invent facts.** Allegations stay allegations. Label figures (estimate, company claim, reported).

## Card fields
Headline · story (3–5 sentences) · most surprising part · what happened afterward · year · original material (footage, ads, documents, and whether it was checked) · sources · why ordinary viewers care · why entrepreneurs care · scores /10 (Entertainment, Narrative, Business relevance, Evidence confidence) · familiarity · careful.

Read `droptopost-research/TASTE.md` first: the owner's verdicts and "Already known" list apply here too.

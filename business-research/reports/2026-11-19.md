## A1. SCAMS & SCANDALS

### A1-1 · Viral 90 · Evidence High · Alleged
**THE FUND PROMISED MORE THAN 6% A MONTH FROM A SECRET CURRENCY-TRADING ALGORITHM. PROSECUTORS SAY 2,000 INVESTORS PUT IN $600 MILLION, AND THE TWO FOUNDERS TOOK $170 MILLION FOR THEMSELVES.**
- **Hook:** A fund with a jokey name in small-town Indiana allegedly ran one of the biggest Ponzi schemes of the year.
- **What happened:** Backward Hat Capital ran funds that said they traded foreign currencies (forex) with a proprietary algorithm and reported returns that "typically exceeded 6% per month" (over 100% a year compounded). Prosecutors in Manhattan say the returns and the strategy were "largely illusory": more than **$200M** of new money paid earlier investors' withdrawals, more than **$130M** went to Phillip Patrick Williams, 51, of Kokomo, and more than **$40M** to Devin Allen Johnson, 29, of Zionsville. When investors tried to pull money out in summer 2026, the fund struggled to pay. The complaint was unsealed on 18 Nov 2026; Williams was arrested in Kokomo and Johnson in Gilbert, Arizona. The SEC and CFTC filed parallel civil cases.
- **Biggest twist:** The "independent" audits weren't independent, prosecutors say: Williams had co-founded a company with the auditor's CEO.
- **Why it could go viral:** A 29-year-old and a 51-year-old, a fund called Backward Hat, a magic algorithm and returns no real trader on earth produces.
- **Why our audience cares:** 6% a month sounds modest to a beginner. It's 100%+ a year, every year, which is the clearest Ponzi signal there is. And a "clean audit" means nothing if you don't know who the auditor is.
- **Financial results:** Raised **>$600M** from **>2,000** investors (prosecutors). Redemptions paid with new money **>$200M**; to Williams **>$130M**, to Johnson **>$40M** (prosecutors). Charges: securities fraud, conspiracy to commit wire fraud, conspiracy to commit securities fraud (max 20 years on the main count). Both are presumed innocent. A blogger, convicted ex-fraudster Barry Minkow, claims the CFTC told him not to talk about the firm before the charges (his own claim, unverified).
- **Audience signals:** not measured (Indiana TV stations, Indiana Lawyer, Sinclair network).
- **Original visuals:** none found (FBI victim page fbi.gov/backwardhatinvestors; local TV arrest coverage).
- **Dates:** withdrawals strained summer 2026 · complaint unsealed and arrests 18 Nov 2026.
- **Sources:** [US Attorney SDNY](https://www.justice.gov/usao-sdny/pr/two-individuals-charged-600-million-ponzi-scheme) · [FOX59](https://fox59.com/news/indycrime/2-indiana-men-backward-hat-capital-founders-charged-in-600-million-ponzi-scheme/) · [The Indiana Lawyer](https://www.theindianalawyer.com/articles/central-indiana-men-charged-in-connection-with-alleged-600m-ponzi-scheme) · [WISH-TV](https://www.wishtv.com/news/local-news/2-indiana-men-charged-in-600-million-investment-fraud-scheme-involving-thousands-of-investors/) · [Local 12](https://local12.com/news/nation-world/phillip-patrick-williams-devin-allen-johns-indiana-cincinnati-on-backward-hat-capital-forex-ponzi-scheme-allegedly-defrauded-more-than-2000-investors-raising-600-million-kokomo)

### A1-2 · Viral 89 · Evidence Medium · Alleged (charges; key figures via press)
**POLAND'S BIGGEST CRYPTO EXCHANGE SAID ITS 4,500 BITCOIN WERE SAFE. THE ONLY MAN WITH THE KEYS DISAPPEARED IN 2022. NOW THE CEO IS COOPERATING WITH PROSECUTORS AND THE OLYMPIC COMMITTEE PRESIDENT HAS BEEN ARRESTED.**
- **Hook:** A missing founder, a "ghost wallet", a $40,000 watch and the country's Olympic chief in a cell.
- **What happened:** Zondacrypto (formerly BitBay) was Poland's largest crypto exchange. Withdrawals slowed from Dec 2025 and the site went offline around 23 Apr 2026. The exchange pointed to a wallet of ~4,500 BTC as proof it was solvent, but CEO Przemysław Kral admitted it couldn't access those coins; founder Sylwester Suszek vanished in March 2022 on his way to a meeting in Czeladź. Prosecutors in Katowice opened a large-scale fraud and money-laundering case; Kral, abroad in Israel, has reportedly been charged and is cooperating in exchange for a lighter sentence. On 27 Aug 2026 the justice minister announced the detention of Radosław Piesiewicz, president of the Polish Olympic Committee, whose general sponsor was Zondacrypto (2026–28 deal).
- **Biggest twist:** Kral's reported testimony could cover how Zondacrypto money was linked to right-wing political figures. Prime Minister Donald Tusk has called it a "Russia-linked" firm bankrolling his opponents.
- **Why it could go viral:** It plays like a thriller: the vanished founder, coins nobody can open, a sports federation's sponsor, and politics.
- **Why our audience cares:** "Proof of reserves" is only proof if someone can actually move the coins. And a famous sponsor's logo on Olympic kit is not due diligence.
- **Financial results:** Customer losses: prosecutors' early estimate **>350M zł (~$96M)**; later reported estimate **≥2.4bn zł (~$650M)** (Onet report, via Cointelegraph). Piesiewicz charges: influence peddling and favouring some creditors over others (Arts. 230 and 302); he allegedly received a **€40,000** watch from Kral, denies the charges and says he paid for it and lost €200,000 himself; three months' pre-trial detention ordered. At least five people charged; none convicted.
- **Audience signals:** not measured.
- **Original visuals:** Zondacrypto CEO's video statement on X (Apr 2026); justice minister Waldemar Żurek's X post announcing the arrest; Olympic sponsorship branding.
- **Dates:** founder vanished Mar 2022 · withdrawals halted Apr 2026 · Olympic chief detained 27 Aug 2026 · CEO cooperation reported late Aug 2026.
- **Sources:** [Notes from Poland](https://notesfrompoland.com/2026/08/27/polish-olympic-committee-chief-detained-over-links-to-collapsed-crypto-firm/) · [The Block](https://theblock.co/news/regulation/2026-08-27-polish-olympic-committee-president-detained-in-zondacrypto-bribery-probe-412961) · [Polskie Radio](https://polskieradio.pl/395/7784/Artykul/3724845,polish-olympic-boss-charged-with-corruption-over-crypto-exchange-links) · [Cointelegraph (CEO seeks leniency)](https://cointelegraph.com/news/zondacrypto-ceo-seeks-leniency-testimony-report) · [crypto.news (founder missing)](https://crypto.news/zondacrypto-collapse-leaves-founder-missing/) · [crypto.news (Tusk)](https://crypto.news/polands-tusk-says-russia-linked-crypto-firm-is-bankrolling-his-opponents/)

### A1-3 · Viral 86 · Evidence High · Verified (company statement + FBI attribution)
**ONE ENGINEER'S LAPTOP WAS HACKED. HOURS LATER $6.3 BILLION HAD LEFT THE WORLD'S BIGGEST CRYPTO EXCHANGE. THE FBI SAYS NORTH KOREA DID IT.**
- **Hook:** The largest crypto theft in history started on a single work computer.
- **What happened:** On 29–30 Oct 2026 attackers drained Binance hot wallets. Binance said a compromised DevOps engineer's laptop let them reach a service container and submit unauthorised withdrawals; it later found "additional points of breach". It paused deposits and withdrawals, put the impact at **~$6.3bn** (TRM Labs: $6.36bn) and pledged to cover user losses from its SAFU fund and company money. Around 1 Nov the FBI linked the theft to North Korean hackers; analysts point to the Lazarus-linked group TraderTraitor. North Korea denies involvement.
- **Biggest twist:** About half the loot was frozen almost immediately, by Tether (~$1.57bn), Circle (>$800M) and partners: "decentralised" money turned out to have off-switches.
- **Why it could go viral:** A record number, a state hacking team and one laptop. Everyone has a work laptop.
- **Why our audience cares:** The weakest point of a billion-dollar business is often one employee's device. Frozen is also not the same as recovered.
- **Financial results:** Impact **~$6.3bn** (Binance estimate; $6.36bn TRM Labs). Frozen on-chain **~$3bn** (Binance; TRM counted ~$2.7bn at its cutoff). Binance says it has covered **~95%** of the impacted amount (company claim). Attribution: FBI assessment, not a court finding.
- **Audience signals:** not measured (ABC stations, BleepingComputer, SecurityWeek, Khaleej Times).
- **Original visuals:** Binance's incident statements; on-chain trackers (TRM Labs, ZachXBT posts).
- **Dates:** theft 29–30 Oct 2026 · FBI attribution ~1 Nov 2026 · later Binance updates Nov 2026.
- **Sources:** [ABC7 Chicago](https://abc7chicago.com/story/binance-estimates-security-breach-impact-63-billion/19999397/) · [Khaleej Times](https://khaleejtimes.com/business/cryptocurrency/crypto-exchange-binance-freezes-assets-after-63-billion-security-breach) · [BleepingComputer (FBI)](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/fbi-links-63-billion-binance-crypto-heist-to-north-korean-hackers/) · [BleepingComputer (laptop)](https://bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/binance-links-63-billion-crypto-theft-to-compromised-devops-laptop) · [SecurityWeek](https://securityweek.com/6-billion-binance-hack-bears-hallmarks-of-north-koreas-tradertraitor)

### A2-1 · Viral 78 · Evidence Medium · Partially verified (deal value from Reuters sources; sales figure reported)
**HE NAMED HIS FIRST BOOT AFTER A CARPENTER FRIEND. FIVE YEARS LATER HIS WORK-BOOT BRAND SELLS $300 MILLION A YEAR, AND DR MARTENS' OLD BACKER JUST BOUGHT IN AT $800 MILLION-PLUS.**
- **Hook:** A boot brand for tradespeople, sold mostly online, went from launch to a $800M+ valuation in about five years.
- **What happened:** Eric Girouard founded Brunt Workwear in North Reading, Massachusetts, in 2020 to make better, cheaper boots for trade workers; its first boot, the Marin, is named after his childhood friend Matt Marin, a foreman carpenter. Brunt raised a $20M Series B led by Stripes in 2022 and Girouard stayed majority owner. In May 2026 Reuters reported it was exploring a sale with JPMorgan. On 18 Nov 2026 Reuters (three sources) reported that Permira and Freeman Spogli are buying a majority stake valuing Brunt at **more than $800M**; Brunt confirmed both firms are investing and that Girouard stays CEO.
- **Biggest twist:** The buyers own the playbook for this: Permira backed Dr Martens to its 2021 IPO, and Freeman Spogli owns Boot Barn.
- **Why it could go viral:** Blue-collar product, a friend's name on the box, a nine-figure payday.
- **Why our audience cares:** An "unsexy" customer (electricians, carpenters) can be the best customer: they wear through boots and talk to each other on site.
- **Financial results:** Annual sales **>$300M** (reported, May 2026 Reuters-syndicated coverage). Valuation **>$800M** (Reuters sources; terms undisclosed). Raised **$20M** Series B (2022). Profit: **unknown**. Stake size: **unknown**.
- **Audience signals:** not measured.
- **Original visuals:** Brunt's own product and job-site videos on its social channels; none checked for this card.
- **Dates:** founded 2020 · sale process May 2026 · deal reported 18 Nov 2026.

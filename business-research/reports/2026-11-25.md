## A1. SCAMS & SCANDALS

### A1-1 · Viral 90 · Evidence High · Verified (jury verdict; appeal announced)
**HUAWEI SIGNED A SECRECY DEAL TO TEST A T-MOBILE ROBOT. PROSECUTORS SAY AN ENGINEER LEFT THE LAB WITH ONE OF ITS ARMS IN HIS BACKPACK. ON TUESDAY A US JURY FOUND HUAWEI GUILTY ON 10 OF 12 COUNTS.**
- **Hook:** The world's best-known Chinese tech company is now a convicted fraudster in a US court, and part of the case is a stolen robot finger.
- **What happened:** On 24 Nov 2026 a federal jury in Brooklyn convicted Huawei Technologies and three subsidiaries on 10 of 12 counts, including conspiracy to steal trade secrets, bank fraud, wire fraud and US sanctions violations; it acquitted Huawei of racketeering conspiracy and of conspiring to obstruct justice. The trial lasted roughly three months. Prosecutors said Huawei ran its Iran business through a company called Skycom and told banks Skycom was only a partner when it was a wholly owned unit. On the trade-secret side, they said Huawei signed a non-disclosure agreement with T-Mobile over "Tappy", a touch-screen testing robot, sent photos and specs to China, and had an employee carry a piece of the robot out of T-Mobile's lab in his backpack. Sentencing is set for 14 Sep 2027; Huawei says it will appeal.
- **Biggest twist:** The case started with the 2018 charges that led to CFO Meng Wanzhou's arrest in Canada. She reached a deal and went home in 2021. The company itself kept fighting, and lost.
- **Why it could go viral:** A $100bn-revenue giant and a robot arm in a backpack. Everyone understands the image.
- **Why our audience cares:** An NDA is only as good as the people in the room. A one-man theft became a criminal conviction for a global company eight years later.
- **Financial results:** Penalties unknown until sentencing; outlets say they could reach **billions of dollars** (estimate, CNN/Reuters). T-Mobile's earlier civil win over Tappy: **$4.8M** jury award (2017, reported). Huawei denies wrongdoing and will appeal.
- **Audience signals:** not measured (CNN, US News/AP, IBTimes, multiple international outlets on 24–25 Nov).
- **Original visuals:** none found (court sketches and the 2018 DOJ indictment press conference; T-Mobile's 2014 lawsuit photos of the Tappy robot).
- **Dates:** conduct 2007–2018 · indictment 2018–2020 · verdict 24 Nov 2026 · sentencing 14 Sep 2027.
- **Sources:** [CNN](https://edition.cnn.com/2026/11/24/china/china-huawei-iran-conviction-intl-hnk) · [US News / AP](https://www.usnews.com/news/best-states/new-york/articles/2026-11-24/chinese-tech-giant-huawei-is-guilty-of-conspiracy-to-steal-trade-secrets-us-jury-finds) · [IBTimes UK](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/huawei-convicted-over-iran-dealings-trade-secrets-1834566) · [US DOJ (2018 charges)](https://www.justice.gov/usao-edny/pr/chinese-telecommunications-conglomerate-huawei-and-huawei-cfo-wanzhou-meng-charged)

### A1-2 · Viral 79 · Evidence High · Alleged (indictment; not-guilty plea)
**HE WAS A VOLUNTEER MEDIC. PROSECUTORS SAY HE TOOK $36.7 MILLION FROM HIS FELLOW MEDICS FOR COVID MASK DEALS THAT NEVER EXISTED, AND GAMBLED $4.7 MILLION OF IT AWAY.**
- **Hook:** In 2020 everyone wanted masks. A trusted community volunteer offered his friends a piece of the trade, with returns of up to 30% in two months.
- **What happened:** Jason Doni Schlessel, 32, of West Hempstead, New York, was arrested on 24 Nov 2026 on an indictment unsealed in Brooklyn federal court. Prosecutors say that between May 2020 and April 2022 he raised about $36.7M from at least 47 investors, promising 8–30% returns within 14–60 days from buying and reselling masks, face shields, gowns and goggles. They say he targeted friends, fellow volunteer medics at Queens Hatzolah and others in the Orthodox Jewish community, showed them fake invoices, purchase orders and falsified bank records, and paid early investors with later investors' money. He is charged with wire fraud and three money-laundering counts; he pleaded not guilty and was released on an unsecured bond.
- **Biggest twist:** The pitch rode on the emergency that the victims, as medics, were working through on the front line.
- **Why it could go viral:** Affinity fraud, a pandemic, and an alleged online casino habit.
- **Why our audience cares:** "Quick-flip" trade deals with short-term high returns are the classic Ponzi shape. Ask to see the goods, not the invoice.
- **Financial results:** Raised **~$36.7M** (prosecutors). Gambling losses **~$4.7M** and personal spending **~$3.5M** (prosecutors). Maximum sentence: up to **50 years** combined (reported). All allegations; presumed innocent.
- **Audience signals:** not measured (News 12 Long Island, VIN News, JTA, Times of Israel live blog).
- **Original visuals:** none found (News 12 TV report).
- **Dates:** alleged scheme May 2020 – Apr 2022 · arrest and arraignment 24 Nov 2026.

## A3. EXTRAORDINARY BUSINESS MODELS (worldwide)

### A3-1 · Model 86 · Evidence Medium · Partially verified (segment figures reported by several outlets; primary filing not opened)
**THE WORLD'S MOST FAMOUS TOILET COMPANY NOW MAKES MORE PROFIT FROM AI CHIP FACTORIES THAN FROM TOILETS.**
- **Hook:** The ceramics know-how behind a Japanese toilet seat turned out to be exactly what chipmakers need to hold silicon wafers in place.
- **The money machine:** TOTO has made toilets since 1917, and it learned to fire precision ceramics at scale. Since the late 1980s it has sold **electrostatic chucks**: ceramic plates that grip a silicon wafer with static electricity inside etching machines, with no clamps touching it. Every new chip layer, especially in stacked NAND memory for AI servers, wears them out and needs more of them, so demand rises with chip output. The unit is ~10% of group sales but, by several reports, more than half of operating profit.
- **Numbers:** Advanced-ceramics unit FY to Mar 2026: **sales ¥67.4bn (+34%)**, **operating profit ¥28.9bn**, **41% margin** (reported via TechTimes / Asia Business Daily; growth rates differ by source). Investment plan: **up to ¥80bn through FY2030**, ¥39bn confirmed (company, Jun 2026, reported). Share price: **+58% in six months** to Feb 2026 (Asia Business Daily); ~18% one-day jump on results (reported). Toilets still bring in most of the revenue.
- **Biggest twist:** TOTO's CTO says the chip-parts division already beats the toilet business on profit. It's the second-largest maker of these chucks in the world (secondary sources).
- **Business lesson:** Your real asset may be a process skill, not your product. Look for a market that pays far more for the same skill.
- **Monetization opportunity:** A content series ("companies secretly in another business": TOTO, Fujifilm cosmetics, Nintendo's playing cards) for clients' channels; an "adjacent-market audit" offer for manufacturing SMEs looking for a high-margin niche for their existing skills.
- **Slovenia/EU angle:** Slovenian precision-ceramics and toolmaking SMEs supply EU fabs; same playbook.
- **Dates:** chuck business since late 1980s · FY results May 2026 · ¥80bn plan 22 Jun 2026.
- **Sources:** [Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/from-toilets-to-chips-totos-electrostatic-chucks-are-becoming-a-crucial-element-of-modern-fabs) · [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/317858/20260606/toto-electrostatic-chuck-unit-outearns-toilet-business-ai-nand-demand-pushes-20-capacity-expansion.htm) · [Asia Business Daily](https://view.asiae.co.kr/en/article/2026021915212903586) · [DIGITIMES](https://www.digitimes.com/news/a20260622VL206.html) · [Fukuoka Now](https://www.fukuoka-now.com/en/news/toto-invests-in-semiconductor-components/fnn_toto-kitakyushu-main-office_2026_cl/)

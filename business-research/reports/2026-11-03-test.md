## A1. SCAMS & SCANDALS

### A1-1 · Viral 93 · Evidence High · Alleged
**THE "AI COMPANY" THAT PROSECUTORS SAY INVENTED 90% OF ITS REVENUE, AND STILL HIT NASDAQ AT $1.4 BILLION**
- **Hook:** Prosecutors say almost every customer of this listed AI company was fake.
- **What happened:** iLearningEngines sold itself as an AI learning platform and listed on Nasdaq via a SPAC in 2024, valued around $1.4B. In September 2024 short seller Hindenburg said its revenue was "largely fake" and that its main "technology partner" was linked to the CEO himself. The company went bankrupt. On 17 April 2026 founder-CEO Harish Chidambaran and ex-CFO Farhan Naqvi were arrested and a 10-count indictment was unsealed in Brooklyn.
- **Biggest twist:** Prosecutors say the money went out to sham "customers" and came straight back in as "revenue". It was a loop, not a business.
- **Why it could go viral:** Peak AI hype meets an old-fashioned round-trip trick, and it's easy to draw on a whiteboard.
- **Why our audience cares:** "AI" in the pitch deck is not due diligence.
- **Financial results:** Reported 2023 revenue **$421M** (company). Prosecutors say **≥90% of it was fabricated**. Valuation at listing ~**$1.4B** (reported). Investor losses: unknown.
- **Audience signals:** not measured.
- **Original visuals:** Hindenburg's report (via [GlobeNewswire summary](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2024/09/09/2943278/32716/en/iLearningEngines-AILE-Tanks-After-Hindenburg-Contends-Revenues-and-Expenses-L-argely-Fake-Hagens-Berman.html)), the Nasdaq listing-day footage.
- **Dates:** Hindenburg report Sep 2024 · indictment unsealed 17 Apr 2026. No plea or trial reported.
- **Sources:** [ACS Information Age](https://ia.acs.org.au/article/2026/ai-firm-allegedly-fabricated--virtually-all--customers--revenue.html) · [CFO Dive](https://www.cfodive.com/news/former-ceo-ex-cfo-ai-company-charged-fraud-ai-revenue-bankruptcy/817986/) · [American Bazaar](https://americanbazaaronline.com/2026/04/22/two-indian-americans-charged-with-multimillion-dollar-ai-platform-scam-479422/)

### A1-2 · Viral 90 · Evidence High · Verified
**HE TOLD INVESTORS HIS AI STARTUP MADE MILLIONS. IT MADE $43,000. NINE YEARS IN PRISON.**
- **Hook:** An Australian "AI marketing" founder raised $39M on revenue that barely existed, and used company money to buy himself a house.
- **What happened:** David Fairfull ran Metigy, a Sydney AI marketing-software startup. Between 2018 and 2021 he gave investors false and misleading statements about the business. Metigy went into administration in July 2022. After an ASIC investigation he pleaded guilty and in June 2026 was sentenced to nine years.
- **Biggest twist:** Revenue was presented as millions. Startup Daily reports the true figure was just over **$43,000**.
- **Why it could go viral:** A tiny real number against a huge claimed one, a prison ending, and an AI label.
- **Why our audience cares:** Founders and investors in marketing tech see exactly how a pitch deck can lie.
- **Financial results:** Investors paid **~A$39M** (ASIC). **A$7.7M** lent by Metigy to buy residential property for his benefit (ASIC). Real revenue just over **$43K** (Startup Daily).
- **Audience signals:** not measured.
- **Original visuals:** none found (ASIC release only).
- **Dates:** misconduct 2018–2021 · collapse Jul 2022 · sentenced Jun 2026 (9 years, non-parole 5 years 4 months).
- **Sources:** [ASIC 26-130MR](https://asic.gov.au/about-asic/news-centre/find-a-media-release/2026-releases/26-130mr-former-metigy-ceo-david-fairfull-sentenced-to-nine-years-imprisonment/) · [Startup Daily](https://www.startupdaily.net/topic/people/metigy-founder-david-fairfull-jailed-for-9-years-for-misleading-conduct-raising-39-million-and-misusing-7-7m/) · [FX News Group](https://fxnewsgroup.com/forex-news/retail-forex/former-ceo-of-ai-marketing-company-metigy-gets-prison-sentence-following-asic-investigation/)

## A3. EXTRAORDINARY BUSINESS MODELS (worldwide)

### A3-1 · Model 91 · Evidence High · Verified
**GROCERIES IN 10 MINUTES: 2,751 DARK STORES, AND NOW IT'S PROFITABLE**
- **Hook:** India's Blinkit delivers milk, phone chargers and iPhones in about 10 minutes, and its order value grew 79% in a year.
- **The money machine:** Small "dark stores" (warehouses with no customers) sit within a couple of kilometres of every customer and stock only fast-selling items. Riders pick up and deliver in minutes. Blinkit earns commissions from brands, delivery and platform fees, and ads. Denser stores mean more orders per store and a lower cost per order.
- **Numbers:** Q2 FY27 (Jul–Sep 2026) net order value **₹20,940 crore (+79% YoY)**. Adjusted EBITDA **₹184 crore** (vs **–₹156 crore** a year earlier). **2,751 stores** (+308 in the quarter). Target 3,500+ by Mar 2027 and ~5,000 by Mar 2028 (company). Parent Eternal net profit ₹125 crore (+92%).
- **Biggest twist:** The thing Western investors called a cash furnace (Getir, Gorillas) became profitable in India.
- **Business lesson:** Speed is a product if density makes it cheap.
- **Monetization opportunity:** (1) A content series: "Why 10-minute delivery failed in Europe and won in India". (2) Advise D2C brands on selling through quick commerce (Blinkit ads and placement). (3) A local hyper-dense "micro-warehouse" model for one category (pharmacy, pet food).
- **Slovenia/EU angle:** European 10-minute players mostly collapsed or merged. That contrast is the story.
- **Dates:** model since 2021–22 · Q2 FY27 results 21 Oct 2026.
- **Sources:** [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2026/10/eternal-q2-net-profit-rises-92-fy27-revenue-up-78-percent) · [Angel One](https://www.angelone.in/news/stocks/eternal-share-price-gains-2-7-as-blinkit-net-order-value-jumps-79-3-in-q2-fy27) · [Kotak Neo](https://www.kotakneo.com/news/stocks/eternal-q2-results-profit-rises-revenue-growth-blinkit-adds-stores/)

### A3-2 · Model 90 · Evidence High · Verified (GMV and share are company claims)
**LIVE-STREAMED AUCTIONS OF CARDS AND SNEAKERS → $20 BILLION**
- **Hook:** Sellers go live, viewers bid in seconds, and the app takes a cut of every sale.
- **The money machine:** Whatnot is QVC for collectors. Sellers stream live, buyers bid in fast auctions, and Whatnot earns a commission plus payment fees on each sale. The show is free content, the scarcity and timer make the sale, and every stream is also marketing for the seller.
- **Numbers:** GMV **~$3B (2024) → $8B (2025) → $8B+ in H1 2026 alone** (company). Series G **$545M at $20B valuation** (Aug 2026), up from $11.5B (Oct 2025). 650,000 new buyers a week, ~60% of US/EU live-shopping market (company claims).
- **Biggest twist:** Live shopping "never worked in the West", and this startup did more GMV in six months than in all of the previous year.
- **Business lesson:** Entertainment plus scarcity plus a timer converts better than a catalogue.
- **Monetization opportunity:** (1) Help niche sellers (vintage, collectibles, local crafts) launch live-auction shows. (2) Sell a "live selling" content playbook to retailers. (3) A content series on the biggest live auction moments.
- **Slovenia/EU angle:** Whatnot operates in parts of Europe. Local sellers could test it.
- **Dates:** founded 2019 · Series G 7 Aug 2026.
- **Sources:** [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/08/07/whatnot-series-g-funding-round-545-million-live-shopping/amp/) · [Net Influencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/whatnot-closes-545m-usd-series-g-at-20b-usd-valuation/) · [TechCrunch (2025 round)](https://techcrunch.com/2025/01/08/livestream-shopping-app-whatnot-raises-265m-pinning-valuation-at-nearly-5b)

### A3-3 · Model 86 · Evidence Medium · Partially verified (volume/revenue are company claims)
**REWARD POINTS FOR PAYING RENT: A $10.75 BILLION COMPANY**
- **Hook:** Rent is everyone's biggest bill and earned nothing, until one startup gave points for it.
- **The money machine:** Renters pay rent through Bilt and earn points (no card fee on rent). Bilt makes money from card spending on everything else, plus landlord partners who use it as a loyalty perk and brands that pay to be redeemed against. It now extends to mortgages (with UWM) and neighbourhood merchants.
- **Numbers:** Raised **$250M at $10.75B valuation** (Jul 2025), up from $3.25B (Aug 2024). Company expected **$100B+** annualized housing spend by end-2025 and **$1B+ revenue by Q1 2026** (company targets, not verified). Another profile: 5M+ members, $45B+ rent/HOA a year (company). EBITDA-profitable in 2023 (company claim).
- **Biggest twist:** The biggest payment in your life was the one no loyalty program touched.
- **Business lesson:** Attach rewards to the spending people can't avoid.
- **Monetization opportunity:** (1) A loyalty program for a "boring" unavoidable payment (utilities, gym, parking) with local merchant partners. (2) Content: "the startup that pays you to pay rent". (3) Loyalty-strategy consulting for property managers.
- **Slovenia/EU angle:** none.
- **Dates:** founded 2019 · $10.75B round Jul 2025 · "Bilt 2.0" card overhaul Jan 2026.
- **Sources:** [FinTech Futures](https://www.fintechfutures.com/venture-capital-funding/bilts-valuation-soars-to-10-75bn-following-250m-funding-round) · [Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/private-equity/bilt-triples-valuation-to-10-8-billion-in-mortgage-push-1) · [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bilt_Rewards)

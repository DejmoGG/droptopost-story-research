## A. SCAMS & SCANDALS

### A1 · 94/100 · Verified
**A MAN SENT $25 MILLION BECAUSE EVERYONE IN HIS VIDEO MEETING WAS FAKE**
- **Hook:** He joined a video call with his CFO and five colleagues. None of them were real.
- **What happened:** A finance worker in the Hong Kong office of British engineering giant Arup (the firm behind the Sydney Opera House) got a message about a confidential transaction. He was suspicious, so he joined a video call to check. The CFO and several colleagues were on it, all AI deepfakes built from public video and audio. He made 15 transfers to five bank accounts.
- **Twist:** The video call he joined to check the request was the scam itself.
- **Why produce it:** Every viewer has been on a video call. Fear plus disbelief, and it's simple to explain.
- **Money:** Loss of HK$200M ≈ **US$25.6M** (Hong Kong police). Arup says its operations and finances weren't affected.
- **Audience signal:** Global coverage (CNN, Fortune). No metrics collected.
- **Dates:** Event January 2024 · Arup named May 2024. No arrests reported in sources found.
- **Sources:** [CNN](https://edition.cnn.com/2024/05/16/tech/arup-deepfake-scam-loss-hong-kong-intl-hnk) · [Fortune](https://fortune.com/europe/2024/05/17/arup-deepfake-fraud-scam-victim-hong-kong-25-million-cfo)
- **Business lesson:** Any payment request needs a callback on a known number, however real the face looks.

- **What happened:** Christine Hunsicker ran CaaStle, a fashion-rental tech startup. Prosecutors say she gave investors fake audited financials, fake bank records and sham documents for years while the company struggled. She pleaded guilty to securities fraud in 2026.

- **Money:** ~$300M raised from investors (DOJ). She agreed to forfeit ~$300M. **Sentence: 5 years** (trade press, Aug 2026).
- **Dates:** Fraud from 2019 · charged July 2025 · plea ~March 2026 · sentenced Aug 2026.
- **Sources:** [US DOJ (SDNY)](https://www.justice.gov/usao-sdny/pr/caastle-founder-charged-300-million-fraud-scheme) · [FashionUnited (sentence)](https://fashionunited.uk/news/people/caastle-founder-sentenced-to-five-years-for-securities-fraud/2026082189935) · [Retail Gazette](https://www.retailgazette.co.uk/blog/2026/08/fashion-tech-founder-jailed-for-five-years-over-300m-investor-fraud/)

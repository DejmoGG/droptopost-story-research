## A3. EXTRAORDINARY BUSINESS MODELS (worldwide)

### A3-1 · Model 92 · Evidence High · Verified
**GOLD PRICED LIKE HERMÈS: THE CHINESE JEWELLER THAT REFUSES TO SELL BY THE GRAM**
- **Hook:** Every other gold shop prices by weight. This one charges a fixed price per piece, and people queued overnight before its price rises.
- **The money machine:** Chinese gold jewellery is a commodity sold by the gram plus a small fee. Laopu Gold sells only handcrafted "ancient-method" pure gold at a fixed price per piece, never discounts, never franchises, and raises prices a few times a year. Each announced increase triggers queues, turning a price hike into a sales event. The result is luxury margins on a commodity.
- **Numbers:** H1 2026 **sales RMB 22.78bn (+60.6%)**, **adjusted net profit RMB 4.32bn (+83.6%)**, **gross margin 41.3%** (company results). FY2025 revenue **~RMB 27.3bn** (reported). Online revenue +171.9% in H1; overseas revenue more than doubled. Premium over market gold reported to have widened to ~1,500 yuan/gram (one report; estimate).
- **Biggest twist:** It's a gold company whose problem is *falling* gold prices: when gold drops, buyers go back to by-the-gram shops, and the queues have already thinned after a 20–30% hike in Feb 2026.
- **Business lesson:** Remove the price comparison (no per-gram price) and a commodity can be sold as a brand.
- **Monetization opportunity:** content series on "commodity → luxury" pricing; a playbook for craft/food producers (honey, olive oil, salt) to stop selling by weight.
- **Slovenia/EU angle:** Laopu is expanding overseas; none in Slovenia.
- **Dates:** H1 results 25 Aug 2026 · latest price increase reported in 2026.
- **Sources:** [HKEX interim results](https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2026/0825/2026082500346.pdf) · [KrASIA](https://kr-asia.com/laopu-gold-stresses-bright-outlook-and-global-plans-as-growth-slows) · [Tiger Brokers news (fixed-price model)](https://www.itiger.com/news/1110703807)

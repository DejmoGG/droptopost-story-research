## A1. SCAMS & SCANDALS

### A1-1 · Viral 84 · Evidence High · Alleged
**HIS FUND CLAIMED IT HAD PREDICTED BREXIT AND TRUMP. PROSECUTORS SAY THE ONE NUMBER HE FAKED WAS HIS OWN PERFORMANCE.**
- **Hook:** A hedge fund that sold "science-based" forecasts from social-media bubbles is now accused of inventing the results it showed investors.
- **What happened:** Vuk Vukovic, a Croatian economist and co-founder of New York-based Oraclum Capital, ran the Orca Bason Fund, marketed on a "wisdom of crowds" and social-network forecasting method (Oraclum says the method called Brexit, Trump 2016 and Biden 2020). On 10 Sep 2026 SDNY prosecutors announced securities and wire fraud charges. They say roughly 20 investors and prospects received statements showing higher returns than the fund administrator's records, including purported brokerage statements showing a +38.44% return and $18.45M NAV where brokerage records showed a 22.46% loss for 2023 and a $2.71M NAV. Croatia's regulator Hanfa had already banned distribution of the fund's units in Croatia (rulings Jul and Dec 2025).
- **Biggest twist:** Prosecutors explicitly don't say the forecasting method was fake, only the track record.
- **Why it could go viral:** "The fund that predicted the future couldn't predict its own audit" is a one-line story.
- **Why our audience cares:** A regional (ex-Yugoslav) founder, a New York fund and a home-country regulator that saw problems first. Always check administrator statements, not the manager's own PDFs.
- **Financial results:** Claimed **+51% gross over two years** (fund marketing, reported by Hedgeweek). Example: **38.44%** shown vs **−22.46%** (2023) in brokerage records (prosecutors, alleged; periods differ per The Deep Dive). Investor losses: **unknown** (not stated in public release). Presumed innocent.
- **Audience signals:** not measured.
- **Original visuals:** SDNY press release; Oraclum's own Substack posts on its forecasting model; Vukovic's Spectator and Seeking Alpha columns.
- **Dates:** fund launched late 2022 / early 2023 · Hanfa bans Jul & Dec 2025 · charges announced 10 Sep 2026.
- **Sources:** [US Attorney SDNY](https://www.justice.gov/usao-sdny/pr/hedge-fund-founder-charged-scheme-misrepresent-fund-performance-investors) · [Hedgeweek](https://www.hedgeweek.com/oraclum-capital-founder-accused-of-falsifying-hedge-fund-returns/) · [The Deep Dive](https://thedeepdive.ca/hedge-fund-founder-who-bet-on-social-media-bubbles-charged-with-faking-returns/) · [Investment Executive](https://www.investmentexecutive.com/news/regulation/hedge-fund-performance-overstated-doj-alleges/) · [Oraclum Substack (method)](https://oraclum.substack.com/p/orca-using-science-to-predict-markets)

### A1-2 · Viral 82 · Evidence High · Alleged
**SHE CO-FOUNDED A LABEL WITH A K-POP STAR AND SIGNED IDOLS FROM EXO AND SHINee. PROSECUTORS SAY SHE TOOK ₩30 BILLION ON DEALS SHE NEVER DELIVERED.**
- **Hook:** The boss of a buzzy K-pop agency is in detention, and her own artists are among those suing her.
- **What happened:** Cha Ga-won co-founded One Hundred Label with singer MC Mong in 2023; its stable included acts such as ChenBaekXi (EXO) and Taemin (SHINee) and the group The Boyz. Police sought her arrest three times from June 2026; she was arrested in early August and indicted in detention around 25 Aug on fraud charges totalling about ₩30bn (~$21M). Prosecutors say an entertainment-tech company paid ₩24.2bn in advances for an artist-IP venture that was never carried out, and they added a ₩5.2bn allegation that artists were made to take out jeonse (housing-deposit) loans. She denied everything at the first hearing on 13 Oct; the trial continued in early November.
- **Biggest twist:** The artists' contracts became the collateral of the alleged fraud; ChenBaekXi and nine members of The Boyz are in separate disputes over unpaid settlements.
- **Why it could go viral:** K-pop fandoms are huge, and "the label boss sold her idols" is instantly understood.
- **Why our audience cares:** Talent IP is an asset you can pre-sell, and that is exactly where an alleged fraud hid.
- **Financial results:** Alleged fraud **~₩30bn** total; **₩24.2bn** advance payments; **₩5.2bn** jeonse-loan allegation (prosecutors, alleged). Her defence: "a default on debt, not fraud", raised during a "hostile M&A".
- **Audience signals:** not measured.
- **Original visuals:** news footage of her arrest-warrant hearing; MC Mong's TikTok lives defending her (reported by SBS Star).

### A1-3 · Viral 78 · Evidence High · Alleged
**HE SOLD INVESTORS "AI TRADING BOTS" PROMISING UP TO 50% IN A MONTH. THE SEC SAYS ONLY 3% OF THE MONEY WAS EVER TRADED, AND NOT BY BOTS.**
- **Hook:** 150 people paid $12.3M for AI bots that, the regulator says, didn't exist.
- **What happened:** Nathan Fuller of Cypress, Texas, founded Privvy Investments and Gateway Digital Investments. The SEC sued him in Houston (announced 30 May 2026), saying that from about Oct 2022 to mid-2024 he raised about $12.3M from roughly 150 investors by claiming proprietary AI bots ran high-frequency crypto arbitrage, with promised returns of 40–50% in 30–45 days. The SEC says only about $380,000 was used to buy crypto, without bots and at no profit; about $6.2M allegedly went on personal spending (luxury cars, jewellery) and $5.5M on Ponzi-like payouts to earlier investors.
- **Biggest twist:** The "AI" was the sales pitch, not the product.
- **Why it could go viral:** "AI bot" is today's magic word for scams, and 3% is a number anyone gets.
- **Why our audience cares:** Ask to see the trades, not the dashboard.
- **Financial results:** Raised **~$12.3M** from **~150** investors; **~$380K** used for crypto; **~$6.2M** personal use; **~$5.5M** Ponzi payments (SEC complaint, alleged). Civil case; outcome unknown.
- **Audience signals:** not measured.
- **Original visuals:** SEC complaint PDF; Privvy's marketing (described in the complaint).
- **Dates:** Oct 2022 – mid-2024 · SEC suit 30 May 2026. (Older than 90 days; included because the "AI bot" angle is current.)

## A3. EXTRAORDINARY BUSINESS MODELS (worldwide)

### A3-1 · Model 85 · Evidence Medium · Partially verified
**CHINA IS PAYING CUSTOMERS IN AI: CREDIT CARDS EARN TOKENS INSTEAD OF MILES, PHONE PLANS SELL THEM LIKE DATA, AND CAFÉS OFFER FREE AI INSTEAD OF WI-FI**
- **Hook:** In China, AI usage has become a loyalty currency.
- **The money machine:** AI labs need users; banks, telcos and shops need a reward that feels new. So Moonshot AI's Kimi and Agricultural Bank of China issued a co-branded card where spending earns Kimi tokens and coding quotas; China Merchants Bank offered new cardholders up to 1.8bn tokens for MiniMax models; the three state telcos sell token bundles like mobile data (from ¥9.9 for 10M tokens). The AI lab gets distribution, the partner gets a cheap, desirable perk, and local governments (Guangzhou's Haizhu district) subsidise companies' token bills.
- **Numbers:** **1.8bn** tokens per new CMB cardholder (bank offer, reported). Telco plans from **¥9.9 / 10M tokens** (reported). Haizhu subsidy **up to ¥2M/year** per company (local policy, reported). Card uptake and revenue: **unknown**.
- **Biggest twist:** The same thing as free Wi-Fi in 2010: compute is becoming the perk that brings customers through the door.
- **Business lesson:** When a new cost becomes something everyone wants, bundling it into an existing product is a distribution channel.
- **Monetization opportunity:** Content series ("AI as currency"); pitch EU banks, telcos or coworking spaces on AI-credit perks; resell API credits bundled with a service.
- **Slovenia/EU angle:** none yet: no EU bank or telco offers this at scale (as far as found).
- **Dates:** programmes launched mid-2026 · Rest of World / TNW coverage Sep 2026.
- **Sources:** [Rest of World](https://restofworld.org/2026/china-ai-tokens-consumer-rewards-credit-cards-telcos-deepseek/) · [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/china-ai-tokens-consumer-products-kimi-credit-card-china-telecom-plans-haizhu-token-loan-european-tokeniser-tax) · [Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/china-banks-carriers-turn-ai-tokens-into-rewards-and-monthly-plans/)

### A3-2 · Model 82 · Evidence Medium · Partially verified
**NO SEATS, NO SUBSCRIPTION: THIS AI COMPANY GETS PAID ONLY WHEN YOUR CUSTOMER'S PROBLEM IS ACTUALLY SOLVED, AND HIT $100M ARR IN 21 MONTHS**
- **Hook:** Software has always charged per user. Sierra charges per result.
- **The money machine:** Sierra, founded in 2023 by Bret Taylor (ex-Salesforce co-CEO, OpenAI chair) and Clay Bavor, builds AI agents that handle customer service for big brands. Instead of per-seat licences, contracts blend a platform fee with a charge per successfully resolved conversation (rates negotiated per contract). If the agent fails and a human has to step in, Sierra doesn't earn that fee, so price is tied directly to the labour cost it replaces.
- **Numbers:** **$100M ARR** in ~21 months (company, Nov 2025, TechCrunch). **~$200M** annualised revenue by May 2026 (reported, not company-confirmed; other estimates ~$150M). Valuation **~$15.8bn** (reported).
- **Biggest twist:** It is effectively a call-centre outsourcer (BPO) priced like one, but with software margins.
- **Business lesson:** Price on the outcome the customer already pays for, and you sell a budget line, not a tool.
- **Monetization opportunity:** Offer agency services on a pay-per-lead/pay-per-booking basis; content on "outcome pricing"; advise SaaS clients on moving from seats to results.
- **Slovenia/EU angle:** none.
- **Dates:** founded 2023 · $100M ARR Nov 2025 · ~$200M reported May 2026.
- **Sources:** [TechCrunch ($100M ARR)](https://techcrunch.com/2025/11/21/bret-taylors-sierra-reaches-100m-arr-in-under-two-years/) · [TechCrunch (outcome pricing)](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/01/saas-in-saas-out-heres-whats-driving-the-saaspocalypse/) · [Sequoia "Inference" (Bret Taylor on pricing)](https://inferencebysequoia.substack.com/p/how-ai-is-reinventing-software-business)

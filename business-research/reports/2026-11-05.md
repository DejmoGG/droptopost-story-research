## A1. SCAMS & SCANDALS

### A1-1 · Viral 86 · Evidence High · Alleged
**ONE CAR LOAN, SEVERAL BANKS: PROSECUTORS SAY A SUBPRIME LENDER PLEDGED THE SAME COLLATERAL AGAIN AND AGAIN UNTIL A $800 MILLION HOLE OPENED**
- **Hook:** JPMorgan, Barclays and Fifth Third lent against car loans that, prosecutors say, had already been promised to someone else.
- **What happened:** Tricolor, a Texas "buy here, pay here" used-car dealer and lender, raised more than $1.9bn through asset-backed bonds from 2020 until it went bankrupt in September 2025. Prosecutors say executives double-pledged loans to several lenders and doctored loan data so defaulted loans looked current. In December 2025 founder Daniel Chu and three executives were charged; the CFO, a finance director and the COO pleaded guilty and are cooperating. On 18 Aug 2026 the SEC filed its own fraud suit. Prosecutors also charged Chu under the rarely used "continuing financial crimes enterprise" (financial-kingpin) statute. His trial was moved from 19 Oct 2026 to 25 Jan 2027.
- **Biggest twist:** Three of the four charged executives are now expected witnesses against their own founder.
- **Why it could go viral:** "Same car, sold to three banks" is a fraud anyone understands in one sentence, and it fooled the biggest names on Wall Street.
- **Why our audience cares:** Collateral checks are only as good as the data the borrower supplies.
- **Financial results:** **>$1.9bn** raised through ABS offerings (SEC, alleged). Collateral hole **~$800M** (SEC complaint, alleged). Chu pleaded not guilty; his lawyer calls the SEC case a "rehash" with many inaccurate allegations.
- **Audience signals:** not measured.
- **Original visuals:** SEC press release and complaint; archive footage of Tricolor lots in Texas and California.
- **Dates:** 2020 – Sep 2025 · bankruptcy Sep 2025 · charges 17 Dec 2025 · SEC suit 18 Aug 2026 · trial set 25 Jan 2027.
- **Sources:** [SEC press release 2026-77](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2026-77-sec-charges-former-executives-fraud-connection-19-billion-collapse-subprime-auto-lender-tricolor) · [American Banker](https://www.americanbanker.com/news/tricolor-ceo-coo-and-cfo-indicted-in-1b-auto-finance-fraud) · [Insurance Journal](https://amp.insurancejournal.com/news/national/2026/08/20/882295.htm) · [Bloomberg (trial date)](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-19/tricolor-founder-daniel-chu-gets-october-trial-on-fraud-charges) · [Inner City Press (trial moved to 25 Jan)](https://innercitypress.com/sdny149lcasteltricolorlimanseiboldicp070726.html)

### A1-2 · Viral 84 · Evidence High · Alleged
**HE DANCED ON STAGE WHILE TEACHING STOCK TRADING. THE REGULATOR SAYS 3.37 LAKH PEOPLE PAID ₹601 CRORE FOR "COURSES" THAT WERE REALLY UNREGISTERED TIPS.**
- **Hook:** India's most theatrical trading teacher filled halls; then the market regulator froze ₹546 crore.
- **What happened:** Avadhut Sathe ran Avadhut Sathe Trading Academy, known for high-energy, dancing seminars. On 4 Dec 2025 SEBI issued an interim order saying the academy's courses were in fact unregistered investment advice and research, banned Sathe, his wife and the academy from the securities market, and impounded ₹546.17 crore as alleged unlawful gains. SEBI says the academy collected about ₹601 crore from around 3.37 lakh (337,000) participants between 2020 and 2025.

- **Why it could go viral:** The seminar footage sells itself, and the number of students is the size of a city.
- **Why our audience cares:** "Education" is the most common wrapper for paid financial advice online; regulators now treat it as advice.
- **Financial results:** Collected **~₹601 crore** from **~3.37 lakh** investors (SEBI, alleged). Impounded **₹546.17 crore** (SEBI order). The academy denies the allegations and says it is a training institute. This is an interim order, not a final ruling; no later development found.
- **Audience signals:** not measured.
- **Original visuals:** his seminar and YouTube clips (widely circulated; not opened here); SEBI interim order.
- **Dates:** 2020 – Oct 2025 · SEBI order 4 Dec 2025 · no final order found as of 5 Nov 2026. (Older than 90 days: included as a rediscovery, since it isn't widely known outside India.)

## A3. EXTRAORDINARY BUSINESS MODELS (worldwide)

### A3-1 · Model 87 · Evidence High · Verified
**INDIA'S MARKETPLACE THAT CHARGES SELLERS 0% COMMISSION, AND STILL TOOK ₹4,132 CRORE IN A QUARTER**
- **Hook:** Every other marketplace takes a cut of each sale. Meesho takes none, and is almost at break-even.
- **The money machine:** Sellers list and sell for free. Meesho earns from what sellers buy *around* the sale: advertising inside the app and shipping through its own logistics network, Valmo. Zero commission brings the cheapest sellers and the most price-sensitive shoppers in small-town India; that volume makes ads and logistics pay.
- **Numbers:** Q2 FY27 (Jul–Sep 2026) revenue **₹4,131.6 crore**, +34% YoY; net loss **₹132.5 crore**, down 68% from ₹411 crore (company results, via BW Disrupt / Indian Startup News). Order growth slowed to **17%** from 55% a year earlier. Listed Dec 2025.
- **Biggest twist:** The loss shrank mainly because of logistics, not ads: denser delivery routes and fewer returns.
- **Business lesson:** Give away the obvious fee, charge for the services the volume makes necessary.
- **Monetization opportunity:** Content series on "free core, paid services" models; advise marketplaces and SaaS clients on moving revenue from take-rate to ads/fulfilment.
- **Slovenia/EU angle:** none.
- **Dates:** zero-commission model since 2021 · IPO Dec 2025 · Q2 FY27 results early Nov 2026.
- **Sources:** [BW Disrupt](https://www.bwdisrupt.com/article/meesho-q2-fy27-loss-narrows-68-to-rs-132-4-cr-revenue-rises-34-631022) · [Indian Startup News](https://indianstartupnews.com/news/meeshos-q2-fy27-loss-narrows-68-to-rs-132-crore-as-revenue-jumps-34-to-rs-4132-crore-12765153) · [Inc42 (model)](https://inc42.com/features/decoding-meesho-business-model-seller-centric-revenue-engine/)

### A3-2 · Model 84 · Evidence High · Verified
**A KOREAN DRUGSTORE CHAIN TURNED INTO A TOURIST ATTRACTION: FOREIGNERS WENT FROM 2% TO 33% OF ITS STORE SALES IN FOUR YEARS**
- **Hook:** Tourists now plan Seoul trips around a pharmacy chain.
- **The money machine:** Olive Young is Korea's dominant health-and-beauty chain. It curates small K-beauty brands, then turns stores in tourist districts into showrooms: 151 "global" stores with multilingual staff, tax-refund flows and tourist-only shopping info. Viral K-beauty on TikTok sends tourists in; the store's curation decides which brands win.
- **Numbers:** Foreign-customer offline sales **₩1 trillion (~$726M)** by Aug 2026, three months earlier than 2025; **+47% YoY** Jan–Aug; foreigners **33%** of offline sales vs **2%** in 2022 (company, via Korea Times / Kyunghyang).
- **Biggest twist:** Growth outside Seoul is faster: Gangwon stores +105% from foreigners.
- **Business lesson:** A retailer that curates a hot category can become the destination, not just a shop.
- **Monetization opportunity:** Retail-as-tourism content; a playbook for EU shops in tourist streets (tax-free flow, multilingual curation, creator-driven bestseller walls).
- **Slovenia/EU angle:** Ljubljana/Bled tourist retail could copy the "curated local bestseller" format.
- **Dates:** foreign surge since 2023 · ₩1tn milestone 31 Aug 2026.
- **Sources:** [Korea Times](https://koreatimes.co.kr/business/companies/20260831/olive-youngs-2026-sales-to-foreign-customers-top-727-mil-by-aug) · [Kyunghyang Shinmun (EN)](https://www.khan.co.kr/en/article/202608311436007) · [Korea Times (repeat visits)](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/business/companies/20260622/foreign-repeat-visits-to-olive-young-sale-surge-11-fold-in-3-years)

## A1. SCAMS & SCANDALS

### A1-1 · Viral 91 · Evidence High · Verified (company release, SEC 8-K, AP/PBS, Al Jazeera, Axios) · UPDATE of 2026-11-18-A1-1
**HE RESIGNED IN DISGRACE ON 12 NOVEMBER. ON 23 NOVEMBER NVIDIA'S BOARD UNANIMOUSLY GAVE HIM HIS JOB BACK.**
- **Hook:** Six days after Nvidia's chair said Jensen Huang was not coming back, the board reinstated him as CEO, after pressure that reached up to the White House.
- **What happened:** Huang stepped down as CEO and left the board on 12 Nov after revelations of affairs, including one with a woman linked to Megaspeed, a Singapore data-centre firm suspected of diverting Nvidia chips to China (shown 12 and 18 Nov). CFO Colette Kress became interim CEO. On 16 Nov CNBC reported the board was not considering bringing him back. Over the following week the stock fell hard: about −14% on Friday 20 Nov and more than −20% for the week, after the Wall Street Journal and New York Times reported an early-stage SEC civil inquiry into whether Nvidia's disclosures misled investors. On 23 Nov the board unanimously reinstated Huang as president, CEO and director; it said it had received input from "senior levels of the United States government" (President Trump had publicly urged his return) and that Huang had been the target of a foreign intelligence effort. Harvey Jones stays chair; Kress returns to CFO. The independent directors' investigation continues.
- **Biggest twist:** The board's own reason for bringing him back is that he was the *target* of a spy operation, the same affair that pushed him out.
- **Why it could go viral:** Fired, a stock crash, a reported SEC probe, a president lobbying, then a comeback in 11 days at the world's most valuable chip company.
- **Why our audience cares:** Key-person risk in one founder, and how fast boards fold when the market and politics push.
- **Financial results:** Weekly loss to 20 Nov **>20%**; Friday 20 Nov **~−14%** (AFP via Yahoo Finance, NST). Market value lost since the early-October peak: **~$1.7trn (~30%)** (Ynet estimate; other outlets give different totals). The Huang family foundation gained **about $2.3bn in a day** on his return (Fortune, 26 Nov). SEC inquiry: **reported, not confirmed** by the SEC or Nvidia; a source told the NYT it may not lead to charges.
- **Audience signals:** not measured (wide global coverage 23–26 Nov).
- **Original visuals:** Nvidia press release and 8-K (links below); no video statement found.
- **Dates:** resignation 12 Nov 2026 · SEC inquiry reported ~20–21 Nov · reinstatement 23 Nov 2026 · Fortune foundation story 26 Nov.
- **Sources:** [NVIDIA press release](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-board-reinstates-jensen-huang-as-president-ceo-and-board-director) · [SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001045810/000104581026000090/nov23pressrelease.htm) · [PBS NewsHour](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/nation/nvidia-board-unanimously-reinstates-jensen-huang-as-ceo-and-chairman) · [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/11/23/nvidia-board-reinstates-jensen-huang-as-ceo) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/23/nvidia-board-jensen-huang-as-ceo) · [Yahoo Finance / AFP (SEC probe)](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/nvidia-faces-us-accounting-probe-215354861.html) · [CNBC (16 Nov: "not considering")](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/16/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang.html) · [Fortune (foundation)](https://fortune.com/2026/11/26/jensen-huang-return-lori-huang-foundation-nvidia-stake/)

### A1-2 · Viral 84 · Evidence High · Verified (court ruling reported by Nation Thailand, Khaosod English, AP)
**THE ONLY SKYSCRAPER IN BANGKOK THAT FELL IN THE EARTHQUAKE. A COURT NOW SAYS ITS CHINESE BUILDER WAS SECRETLY OWNED THROUGH THAI FRONT MEN.**
- **Hook:** 33 storeys came down in seconds on 28 March 2025 and killed about 92 people. Nineteen months later the builder's boss is going to prison, not for the collapse, but for who really owned the company.
- **What happened:** The State Audit Office headquarters, under construction by a joint venture that included China Railway No. 10 (Thailand), was the only high-rise in Bangkok to collapse in the Myanmar earthquake. Investigators then found the Thai arm was majority-held on paper by Thai shareholders acting as nominees, a way around the Foreign Business Act. On 18 Nov 2026 Bangkok's Criminal Court sentenced Chinese director Chuanling Zhang to three years in prison and a 1 million baht fine; three Thai shareholders were jailed too and ordered to give up their shares. The court rejected the company's claim that it hadn't done construction work and Zhang's claim to be a Chinese state official. A separate trial over the collapse itself is still running.
- **Biggest twist:** The first prison sentence from a 92-death disaster is for paperwork: a fake ownership structure.
- **Why it could go viral:** Everyone saw the collapse video. Few know what was found behind the company afterwards.
- **Why our audience cares:** Nominee shareholding is common in Southeast Asia; it can sink a company and its local front men in court.
- **Financial results:** Fine **1 million baht** (court). Contract value, losses: not stated in sources used. Reports differ on whether the company was ordered dissolved or to stop operating.
- **Audience signals:** not measured. Collapse footage was viewed worldwide in March 2025 (counts not measured).
- **Original visuals:** CCTV and phone footage of the collapse (28 Mar 2025, widely broadcast); court-day photos in Thai press.
- **Dates:** collapse 28 Mar 2025 · Zhang arrested 19 Apr 2025 · sentence 18 Nov 2026.
- **Sources:** [Nation Thailand](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/general/40073635) · [Khaosod English](https://www.khaosodenglish.com/news/2026/11/18/chinese-director-thai-nominees-jailed-over-sao-building-collapse/) · [AP via La Nación](https://www.lanacion.com.ar/usa/a-chinese-executive-is-convicted-in-a-case-linked-to-a-bangkok-building-collapse-nid18112026/) · [Pattaya Mail](https://www.pattayamail.com/thailandnews/thai-court-jails-chinese-executive-for-three-years-over-collapsed-sao-building-572363)

## A2. SUCCESSFUL BUSINESSES

### A2-1 · Viral 76 · Evidence Medium · Partially verified (founding story widely reported, figures differ by source; 2025–26 numbers via Bloomberg and company bond release)
**A KURDISH SHEPHERD'S SON SAW A "FOR SALE" AD FOR A DEAD YOGURT FACTORY. HE BOUGHT IT WITH A SMALL-BUSINESS LOAN. IT'S NOW A $20 BILLION COMPANY.**
- **Hook:** Kraft was closing a 1920s plant in upstate New York. A 33-year-old cheesemaker who had never run a big factory bought it for less than the price of a house in many cities.
- **What happened:** Hamdi Ulukaya, who grew up in a dairy family in eastern Turkey, came to the US to study and ran a small feta business. In Aug 2005 he bought the shut plant in South Edmeston, NY, with a US Small Business Administration loan (reported as $700K–$800K; Fortune says just over $1M with Key Bank), hired some of the laid-off workers, and spent two years perfecting a strained Greek-style yogurt. Chobani shipped its first cases in Oct 2007 and passed $1bn annual revenue about five years later. In Oct 2025 it raised $650M at a $20bn valuation; 2025 revenue rose ~29% to $3.8bn, helped by protein and GLP-1 diet demand (Bloomberg). In Aug 2026 it reportedly trimmed its 2026 earnings forecast to ~$920M from $1bn+.
- **Biggest twist:** A giant's discarded factory became the base of the brand that took Greek yogurt from niche to the top of US supermarket shelves.
- **Why it could go viral:** The "for sale" flyer, the empty factory, the old workers, and a 20-year arc to $20bn.
- **Why our audience cares:** Buying an incumbent's unwanted capacity cheaply can be a faster start than building from zero.
- **Financial results:** Revenue **$3.8bn (2025, +29%)** (Bloomberg). Valuation **$20bn post-money** (Oct 2025 round, raised $650M). 2026 earnings guidance **~$920M** (reported Aug 2026; earlier "over $1bn"). Founder stake: **just under 90%** (Bloomberg, per prospectus; other sources say ~68%). Loan size: sources differ ($700K–$1M+).
- **Audience signals:** not measured.
- **Original visuals:** Ulukaya's 2019 TED talk "The anti-CEO playbook" (not checked today); factory footage in many TV features.
- **Dates:** plant bought Aug 2005 · first sales Oct 2007 · $20bn round Oct 2025 · bond Apr 2026 · forecast cut Aug 2026.
- **Note:** a rediscovery story (window 4), not breaking news; the founding tale is well known in the US.
- **Sources:** [Bloomberg (Mar 2026)](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-06/yogurt-maker-chobani-expects-over-1-billion-in-2026-earnings) · [Bloomberg feature (Apr 2026)](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2026-04-21/greek-yogurt-maker-chobani-s-expansion-boosted-by-protein-glp-1-craze) · [Entrepreneur (founding)](https://www.entrepreneur.com/article/223668) · [Fortune (2013)](https://fortune.com/2013/06/10/the-yogurt-company-growing-as-fast-as-google-and-facebook/) · [PR Newswire ($800M notes)](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/chobani-announces-upsizing-and-pricing-of-its-800-million-private-offering-of-senior-notes-302738780.html)

## A3. EXTRAORDINARY BUSINESS MODELS (worldwide)

### A3-1 · Model 80 · Evidence High · Verified (company IR figures; analyst summaries for the factory count)
**A SUPERMARKET THAT OWNS ITS OWN FACTORIES, SELLS RESTAURANT-SIZE PACKS TO HOUSEWIVES, AND KEEPS BREAKING PROFIT RECORDS.**
- **Hook:** Japan's "Gyomu Super" (literally "business supermarket") was meant for restaurants. Families turned it into a cult, and the parent company makes the food itself.
- **The money machine:** Kobe Bussan franchises over 1,100 Gyomu Super stores, so franchisees carry the store costs while the company earns as wholesaler. About a third of sales are its own-brand products made in ~25 group food factories in Japan or imported directly, so the company keeps the manufacturer's margin and the retailer's. Huge packs, a narrow range and bare stores keep costs low and prices below normal supermarkets.
- **Numbers:** FY to Oct 2025: **net sales ¥551.7bn, operating profit ¥39.8bn, both records** (company IR). Own-brand share **~36% of sales**, **25 domestic factories** (analyst estimate, Envalith). Stores **1,100+** (company slides). FY2026 guidance: **operating profit ¥43.0bn** (Q2 summary). FY2026 results due mid-Dec 2026.
- **Biggest twist:** A wholesale format for restaurants became one of Japan's favourite family stores during the cost-of-living squeeze.
- **Business lesson:** Owning manufacturing lets a discounter win on price and still earn two margins.
- **Monetization opportunity:** A "factory-to-shelf" explainer series; advise EU private-label food makers on a vertically integrated discount format; a playbook for franchise-wholesale economics.
- **Slovenia/EU angle:** none (the closest EU parallel is private-label-heavy discounters).
- **Dates:** chain founded 2000 · FY2025 results Dec 2025 · FY2026 guidance (Q2) mid-2026 · next results ~Dec 2026.
- **Sources:** [Kobe Bussan IR](https://kobebussan.co.jp/english/ir/) · [Investing.com (Q3 profit +26%)](https://ng.investing.com/news/earnings/kobe-bussan-reports-26-rise-in-q3-operating-profit-beats-estimates-93CH-2101149) · [Envalith (segments)](https://envalith.com/en/companies/3038/segments) · [Quartr (Q2 2026 summary)](https://quartr.com/events/kobe-bussan-co-ltd-3038-q2-2026_FsLjNSsh)

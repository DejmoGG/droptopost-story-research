# REPORT A — Business & investment stories

**Mission:** "Expose the scams. Discover businesses that actually make money. Find opportunities worth building."

Run procedure, shared rules, scoring and card format: root `CLAUDE.md`. Format reference: `reports/2026-11-03.md` (first run; it used the old labels A/B/C = today's A1/A2/A3).

## Categories (up to 10 each, ranked)

**A1. Scams & scandals.** Financial fraud, investment scams, Ponzi schemes, fake startups, exposed influencers, corporate deception, extraordinary collapses, people losing fortunes through decisions that seemed brilliant. Identifiable people, real events, big consequences, a twist. Best sources: SEC, US DOJ, FTC, UK SFO/FCA, Europol, court reporting, Reuters, Bloomberg.

**A2. Successful businesses.** Ordinary founders, strange or simple businesses with big money, one-video breakthroughs, started-with-nothing stories, overlooked products, unusual subscriptions, clever marketing. Explain the business model. Keep revenue, profit, cash flow and valuation separate.

**A3. Business opportunities abroad → Slovenia.** Proven models from the US, Japan, Korea, China, the rest of Europe and elsewhere that are rare in Slovenia: repeat demand, recurring revenue, low startup capital, growth through social media, a real edge from our media engine. **Don't reject a fascinating model just because its numbers aren't public. Mark them "unknown".** Each card adds:
- **Business model**: what exactly is sold.
- **Who pays, and why.**
- **Financial results**: verified figures, or "unknown". Brochure, franchise and listing numbers are labelled as claims.
- **Startup capital**: documented, estimated (say so), or unknown.
- **Slovenia potential**: first assessment + the biggest unknowns (existing competitors, regulation, willingness to pay). Check AJPES or Slovenian sources when quick.
- **Opportunity score /100**: separate from the viral score. Covers evidence of paying customers, recurring demand, capital and difficulty, local competition, margins, our media advantage, regulatory risk. A viral story is not an investment case.

## Report file
`business-research/reports/YYYY-MM-DD.md`:
- Header (see `CLAUDE.md`)
- `## TOP 5`
- `## A1. SCAMS & SCANDALS`
- `## A2. SUCCESSFUL BUSINESSES`
- `## A3. BUSINESS OPPORTUNITIES (could they work in Slovenia?)`
- Optional: `## Dropped` (one line each)
- Optional: `## Follow-up video ideas`

Card IDs: `A1-1 … A1-10`, `A2-1 …`, `A3-1 …`. History/index ID = `YYYY-MM-DD-A1-1`.

## Not wanted in Report A
Marketing and AI-content stories belong in **Report B**. If a story fits both, put it in only one, the one it serves better, and index it once.

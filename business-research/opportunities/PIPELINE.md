## Where Slovenia's real needs are (the starting point)
- **Home care for older people can't keep up.** Under the new long-term care law (in force since 1 Dec 2025), **1,148 people chose the cash benefit while only 235 received home care** between December and February. The reason: providers lack carers (Reported, [N1](https://n1info.si/novice/slovenija/najtezje-pricakovana-pravica-zaenkrat-ostaja-prakticno-nedosegljiva/), ZZZS data). The cash benefit is **€89–491/month** (Reported, [N1](https://n1info.si/novice/slovenija/se-bolj-splaca/), [gov.si](https://www.gov.si/zbirke/projekti-in-programi/dolgotrajna-oskrba/pravice-in-storitve/)). Home care is getting scarcer and more expensive (Reported, [RTV SLO](https://www.rtvslo.si/slovenija/za-zagotavljanje-oskrbe-na-domu-primanjkuje-predvsem-kadra-postaja-pa-tudi-vse-drazja/667321)).
- **Doctors:** **134,737 insured people had no personal doctor** on 31 Mar 2026 (Verified, ZZZS via [Svet24](https://svet24.si/zdravje/134000-ljudi-brez-osebnega-zdravnika-1892975), [24ur](https://www.24ur.com/novice/slovenija/dostop-do-osebnega-zdravnika-brez-kar-136251-oseb.html)).
- **Tourism keeps growing but is short of staff.** 2025 was a record, with about **17.8M overnight stays (+6%)** (Verified, [gov.si](https://www.gov.si/novice/2026-01-07-slovenski-turizem-v-2025-ob-zmerni-rasti-v-ospredju-kakovost-trajnost-in-dolgorocna-odpornost/)). Staff shortage is among the top risks for 2026 (Reported, [STO barometer](https://www.slovenia.info/sl/novinarsko-sredisce/novice/38456-barometer-slovenskega-turizma-stabilna-pricakovanja-za-leto-2026-in-optimisticna-napoved-poletne-sezone)).
- **New short-term rental law (ZGos-1):** 14 municipalities, including **Ljubljana, Bled, Piran, Bohinj and Kranjska Gora**, are classed as high-risk. There, short-term renting is capped at **60 days/year in 2027–28**, unless the municipality set 30–270 days by 31 Oct 2026. On top of that, more than 75% of the building's co-owners must consent (Verified, [gov.si](https://www.gov.si/novice/2026-05-08-objavljen-sklep-o-dolocitvi-obcin-z-visokim-tveganjem-negativnih-vplivom-kratkotrajnega-najema/), [Unija](https://unija.com/sl/drzavni-zbor-potrdil-nov-zakon-o-gostinstvu/)).
- **Labour:** foreign citizens were **16% of all employed** in H1 2025. Employers are short of electricians, cooks, drivers and welders, and the shortage is called "long-term and structural" (Reported, [ZRSZ](https://www.ess.gov.si/fileadmin/user_upload/Trg_dela/Dokumenti_TD/Analize/Strokovna_izhodisca_za_2026.pdf), [Svet24](https://svet24.si/novice/slovenija/vpis-srednja-sola-analiza-poklici-trg-dela-1898967)).
- **Energy:** about **3,200 heat pumps installed a year and ~45,000 in use** (Reported, EurObservER via [Žurnal24](https://www.zurnal24.si/pod-streho/varcna-hisa/kurilno-olje-in-zemeljski-plin-se-poslavljata-prihajajo-te-naprave-357200); the year isn't stated). The Eko sklad subsidy for air-to-water heat pumps is up to 20%, max €1,000 (Reported, [Žurnal24](https://www.zurnal24.si/pod-streho/varcna-hisa/spremembe-pri-subvencijah-za-toplotne-crpalke-in-kotle-na-lesno-biomaso-403488)).

### OPP-01 · Investment 79 · Storytelling 70 · Watchlist
**PRIVATE HOME SUPPORT FOR OLDER PEOPLE, PAID BY THE FAMILY, BOOKED BY THE HOUR**
- **Customer and problem:** Adult children (40–65) whose parent needs help at home. Public home care has no carers, so people get fewer hours than they're entitled to, or take the cash benefit instead (1,148 vs 235, above).
- **The business:** Non-medical help: personal care, household help, company, escort to the doctor, evenings and weekends. Sold as a weekly plan (e.g. 3 × 2 h).
- **Foreign proof:** **Home Instead** (US, a franchise in 14+ countries): **$2.1B in system-wide sales** and 1,089 units in 2020, bought by Honor in 2021 (Verified, [Franchise Times](https://www.franchisetimes.com/franchise_mergers_and_acquisitions/at-2-1b-in-sales-home-instead-founders-sell-to-honor-technology/article_1af75bd4-fa17-11eb-9bdd-4f018704f8da.html), [Home Health Care News](https://homehealthcarenews.com/2021/08/honor-to-aquire-home-instead-creating-2-billion-home-care-services-company/)). Austria's 24-hour model: about 23,000 households receive up to €800/month in state support (Reported, [Sozialministerium](https://www.sozialministerium.gv.at/Themen/Pflege/24-Stunden-Betreuung.html), Hilfswerk). Proof that the need is paid for, **not** proof that Slovenians will pay.
- **Pricing and how often it's paid:** The public service's real cost is **€26.50/h**, but users pay **€7.95/h** after subsidy (Verified, [Uradni list, Dol pri Ljubljani 2024](https://www.uradni-list.si/glasilo-uradni-list-rs/vsebina/2024-01-1768/sklep-o-soglasju-k-novi-ceni-socialno-varstvene-storitve-pomoc-druzini-na-domu-v-obcini-dol-pri-ljubljani)). Private price: **€24–30/h (Estimate)**. Paid weekly, recurring. One client at 6 h/week ≈ €650–780/month (Estimate). The cash benefit (€89–491) can go towards it.
- **Startup and running costs:** The ministry's work permit for social care services is needed for personal care, with a qualified head and a 2-year financial plan (Verified, [SPOT](https://spot.gov.si/sl/dejavnosti-in-poklici/dovoljenja/dovoljenje-za-delo-opravljanje-socialnovarstvenih-storitev-ki-ga-izda-ministrstvo-za-solidarno-prihodnost/)). Main costs: carers' wages, a care coordinator, insurance, cars. **€60–120K to the first ~25 clients (Estimate).**
- **Slovenian competitors and substitutes:** The public municipal provider (ZOD Ljubljana) and concession holders under the new law. **Pristan**, a private non-profit and the largest private provider in Ljubljana. Informal cash-in-hand carers. Care homes, which have waiting lists.
- **What they fail to solve:** Not enough hours, no evenings or weekends, no choice of carer, slow start.
- **Why customers would pay more:** The public option isn't available. Guilt and distance (children working or abroad). Paying more lets us pay carers more, which is how we'd win staff.
- **Our edge:** Trust is the bottleneck on **both** sides: families and carers. DropToPost content (real carers, real routines, "how to get your entitlement") wins both. That's a real advantage over non-profits and public providers who don't market.
- **Biggest risks:** **Carer supply.** The shortage that breaks the public system can break ours too. Margin pressure (staffing businesses usually net ~5–10%: Estimate). Exactly which tasks need the permit (Unknown: ask the ministry). Reputational damage from one bad incident.
- **Why now:** The care law launched in Dec 2025 and isn't working yet (Pristan's director expects another year, per N1). Families are newly aware of their entitlements, and many hold cash benefits.
- **Validation test (4 weeks, about €1.5K):** (1) Demand: a landing page plus 3 videos aimed at adult children in Ljubljana, offering a free home assessment and a first block at €25/h. Pass = **30 assessment requests and 8 paid bookings**. (2) Supply: a hiring ad at €2–3/h above public pay. Pass = **15 qualified carer applicants**. Both must pass.
- **Score breakdown:** demand 14 · margin 8 · recurring 10 · market 7 · capital 8 · differentiation 6 · media 8 · partner 8 · proof 5 · why now 5 = **79**

### OPP-02 · Investment 76 · Storytelling 60 · Watchlist
**"EVERY ENQUIRY ANSWERED IN 5 MINUTES": A LEAD-RESPONSE AND BOOKING DESK FOR HIGH-VALUE LOCAL BUSINESSES**
- **Customer and problem:** Clinics (dental, aesthetic, physio), renovation, solar and heat-pump installers, car dealers and real estate agents. They pay for leads, then answer late or never. Classic study: 2,241 US firms took **42 hours on average** to answer a web lead, and answering within 1 hour made qualifying the lead **~7× more likely** (Reported, [HBR 2011 via BYU](https://scholarsarchive.byu.edu/facpub/9711)). **Not measured in Slovenia yet.**
- **The business:** Done for the client: AI plus human responders answer calls, forms and DMs within 5 minutes in Slovenian, qualify the lead and book the appointment in the client's calendar. Paired with DropToPost content that creates the leads.
- **Foreign proof:** **Hatch** (US, AI lead response for home services): **~$25M ARR in Nov 2025, growing ~70% a year**; Yelp agreed to buy it for **~$270M** in Jan 2026 (Verified, [Business Wire / Yelp](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260121295483/en) · [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/yelp-bets-270m-on-ai-with-hatch-acquisition-to-automate-services-leads/)). Podium claims $100M in AI ARR (Claim, ignored).
- **Pricing and how often it's paid:** A monthly fee of €390–990 plus €15–40 per booked appointment (Estimate). Monthly and recurring.
- **Startup and running costs:** Software (an off-the-shelf CRM plus a voice/text AI layer), 1–2 responders, an ops lead. **€25–60K (Estimate).** High gross margin once automated.
- **Slovenian competitors and substitutes:** Marketing agencies (they sell leads, not conversion). Call-centre outsourcers. CRM resellers. The business's own receptionist. Nobody found who sells "content + response + booking" paid per result (Unknown: needs a proper search of bizi.si).
- **What they fail to solve:** Agencies stop at the click, and receptionists are busy or offline after 4 pm.
- **Why customers would pay more:** Paying per booked appointment makes the ROI visible, and they lose fewer of the leads they already pay for.
- **Our edge:** The strongest of any candidate. It extends what DropToPost already sells, and the results make content: "we tested 60 Slovenian clinics".
- **Biggest risks:** Slovenian-language voice AI quality (Unknown). Small businesses churn when budgets tighten. Easy to copy in software terms, so the moat is the bundle with content plus case studies. Small market: Slovenia has a limited number of high-value local businesses (Estimate).
- **Why now:** Voice and text AI became cheap enough in 2025–26, and Hatch's exit shows the category is paying.
- **Validation test (1 week, about €0):** Mystery-shop **60 Slovenian businesses** (12 each in dental, aesthetic, installers, car dealers, real estate): send a web enquiry and make one call. Measure time to first reply and missed calls. Pass = **median > 4 h or > 30% never reply**. Then offer **5 paid pilots** at €490/month. Pass = 3 sign. The mystery shop is also a strong video.
- **Score breakdown:** demand 7 · margin 13 · recurring 9 · market 6 · capital 9 · differentiation 7 · media 10 · partner 7 · proof 4 · why now 4 = **76**

### OPP-03 · Investment 71 · Storytelling 65 · Watchlist
**AFTER THE AIRBNB CAP: MANAGING FURNISHED 1–12 MONTH RENTALS FOR OWNERS IN LJUBLJANA AND ON THE COAST**
- **Customer and problem:** Owners of short-term rental flats in the 14 high-risk municipalities. From 2027 they face the 60-day cap and the 75% co-owner consent rule, so they need a legal way to keep high yields without the work.
- **The business:** Full management of furnished mid-term stays for relocating employees, foreign specialists, students and remote workers. A fee of 15–25% of rent (Estimate).
- **Foreign proof:** **Wunderflats** (Berlin): **$21M growth round in 2021**, about 5,000 companies booking (Reported and Claim, [Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20210719005104/en/Wunderflats-Receives-$21-Million-in-Growth-Funding-to-Make-It-Easier-for-People-to-Access-Housing-in-Europe) · [CREtech](https://cretech.com/news/capital-markets/wunderflats-receives-21-million-in-growth-funding-to-make-it-easier-for-people-to-access-housing-in-europe)). Revenue unknown, so this is weak proof.
- **Market size:** About 1,700 active Airbnb listings in Ljubljana (Estimate, third-party [Airbtics](https://airbtics.com/annual-airbnb-revenue-in-ljubljana-slovenia-slovenia)), plus Bled and the coast. Small.
- **Startup and running costs:** Low. A small team, cleaning partners, a booking site. **€20–40K (Estimate).**
- **Competitors:** Long-term rental estate agencies. Short-term rental managers who will pivot. Platforms such as Flatio (whether it's active in Slovenia: Unknown).
- **Biggest risks:** **Whether stays over 30 nights fall outside ZGos-1** (Unknown, and decisive: read the law's definition). Whether Ljubljana adopted its own day limit by 31 Oct (Unknown). Tenant demand is unmeasured.
- **Why now:** The cap starts on 1 Jan 2027, so owners will decide in the next 2 months. Content like "What happens to your Airbnb in 2027" lands now.
- **Validation test (3 weeks, about €500):** One explainer video plus a free owner guide. Pass = **40 owner leads**. List 3 willing units mid-term. Pass = **10 qualified tenant enquiries per unit**.
- **Score breakdown:** demand 8 · margin 10 · recurring 9 · market 5 · capital 9 · differentiation 6 · media 8 · partner 8 · proof 3 · why now 5 = **71**

### OPP-06 · Investment 63 · Storytelling 60 · Investigating
**PRIVATE GP MEMBERSHIP FOR PEOPLE WITHOUT A PERSONAL DOCTOR**
- **Demand:** 134,737 without a personal doctor; about 27–38K of them in Ljubljana (Reported, [Svet24](https://svet24.si/zdravje/134000-ljudi-brez-osebnega-zdravnika-1892975), [Delo tag](https://www.delo.si/tag/zd-ljubljana)).
- **Why it's held back:** The partner would have to be a doctor, and doctors are exactly what's scarce. Decisive unknown: whether a private doctor outside the ZZZS network can issue public referrals, sick leave and prescriptions. If not, the value is small.
- **Next check:** ZZZS rules for private doctors without a concession, and private price lists in Ljubljana.

### OPP-08 · Investment 62 · Storytelling 80 · Investigating
**CONTENT-LED EXPORT BRAND FOR A SLOVENIAN FOOD PRODUCT (E.G. PREKMURJE PUMPKIN SEED OIL) INTO GERMAN-SPEAKING MARKETS**
- **Foreign proof:** **Graza** (US olive oil sold in squeeze bottles, built on social content): **~$48–60M in 2024 sales** (Reported by secondary sources, [Olive Oil Times citing Bloomberg](https://www.oliveoiltimes.com/business/u-s-consumers-are-embracing-olive-oil-in-squeeze-bottles/135372)).
- **Why it's held back:** Austrian Styrian pumpkin seed oil, with its protected-origin label, dominates the category. Food margins after logistics and retail are thin. Slovenian demand doesn't matter here: it's an export bet.
- **Next check:** Prices and volumes of Prekmurje producers, Amazon.de category sales, and producers willing to make a private label.

### OPP-04 · Investment 53 · Rejected
**TURNKEY ENERGY RENOVATION (HEAT PUMP + INSULATION + SOLAR + SUBSIDY PAPERWORK)**
- **Foreign proof is strong:** **Thermondo** had **€123.0M revenue in 2024** and 783 staff (Verified, [Bundestag lobby register](https://www.lobbyregister.bundestag.de/media/6b/a0/751189/thermondo-GmbH_JA2024_LobbyRgstr.pdf)), and reached operating break-even (Reported, [Börsen-Zeitung](https://www.boersen-zeitung.de/unternehmen-branchen/thermondo-erreicht-operativen-break-even)). **Enpal** had **€905M revenue in 2023** (Claim, [Enpal](https://corporate.enpal.com/pressemitteilungen/enpal-achieves-revenues-of-eur-905-million-in-2023-and-records-a-positive-operating-cash-flow)).
- **Why it fails in Slovenia:** Only ~3,200 heat pumps a year. Small subsidies. Strong incumbents (GEN-I Sonce, Petrol, Termo Shop, manufacturer installer networks). Electricians are scarce. Project-based, not recurring. Heavy on crews and capital.
- **Maintenance-subscription variant rejected:** about 45K units, and manufacturers such as Bosch require their own authorised servicer to keep the warranty (Verified, [Bosch SI](https://www.bosch-homecomfort.com/si/sl/stanovanjski/storitve/tehnicna-pomoc-in-servis/)).
